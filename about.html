About Yadawi

Yadawi means "handmade" in Arabic. It is an online marketplace where customers in the UAE can buy handmade jewelry, decorations, candles, accessories and gifts directly from local artisans.

Why we built it

Most UAE artisans sell through Instagram messages or weekend markets. Customers cannot search, compare prices or read reviews in one place. Yadawi puts the products, the stock and the reviews on one website.

How it works

Browse the shop, add products to your cart, register and check out. You can review any product after you log in. Each customer can post one review per product.

Student project notice

This website is the final project for INT424 E-Commerce at Ajman University. The products are sample data. No real payments are taken and no real deliveries are made.

Technology

HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the website. Supabase for the database, user accounts and the REST API. GitHub Pages for hosting.
